ABOUT SCRAPSETU

Technology that makes scrap
valuation simpler.

ScrapSetu is a project that combines a clean digital experience with machine-learning based scrap price estimation.

OUR PURPOSE

Make scrap value easier to understand.

Users can enter the material, quality, weight, location and date of their scrap and receive an estimated value from the prediction model.

The platform is designed to grow into a complete recycling marketplace with pickup booking, user accounts, order tracking and an administrator dashboard.
